SOC L1 INCIDENT REPORT
Incident: Suspicious Phishing Email Analysis

1. INCIDENT SUMMARY
A fictional suspicious email was examined in a controlled
SOC training exercise. The email used urgent language and
requested account verification.

2. INVESTIGATION DETAILS
Sender: support@company-security.test
Reply-To: verify-account@suspicious-mail.test
Subject: URGENT: Your Account Will Be Suspended

3. EMAIL AUTHENTICATION
SPF: FAIL (simulated)
DKIM: NONE (simulated)
DMARC: FAIL (simulated)

4. INDICATORS OF COMPROMISE
URL: https://example.com/account/verify
IP: 192.0.2.45

Note: These are reserved training indicators and are
not confirmed malicious IoCs.

5. FINDINGS
- Sender and Reply-To domains differ.
- Urgency-based language was used.
- Authentication results indicate failures in the sample.
- The verification URL requires investigation in a real case.

6. RISK ASSESSMENT
Medium risk for this training scenario.
No actual compromise or credential theft was demonstrated.

7. RECOMMENDED RESPONSE
- Do not click suspicious links.
- Verify the sender through a trusted channel.
- Report and quarantine suspicious messages.
- Block confirmed malicious indicators according to policy.
- Review related email and authentication logs.

8. CONCLUSION
The exercise demonstrated email-header inspection,
phishing-indicator identification, IoC extraction and
basic SOC L1 incident documentation.

Environment: Controlled cybersecurity training lab.
